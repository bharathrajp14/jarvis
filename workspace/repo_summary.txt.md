# Repository Overview
The BR-Jarvis repository is a sophisticated, multi-modal AI operating system featuring an extensive suite of agents, interfaces, and tools.

## Key Components
* **Core & Orchestration:** `core/`, `orchestrator/`, `agent/`, `multi_agent/` - Handlers for AI logic, coordination, and loop control.
* **Memory & Context:** `memory/`, `memory_db/`, `context/` - Persistent and short-term operational memories.
* **Tools & Capabilities:** `tools/`, `skills/`, `actions/`, `plugins/`, `connectors/` - System and third-party integrations.
* **Perception & I/O:** `vision/`, `voice/`, `computer/`, `screen_server/` - Multi-modal capabilities.
* **Interfaces:** `ui/`, `desktop_ui/`, `dashboard/`, `web/`, `mobile/` - Frontends for cross-platform access.
* **Security:** `guardian/`, `security/`, `redteam/`, `permissions.py` - Policy enforcement and safety guardrails.
* **Documentation & Planning:** Multiple `ARTIFACT_*.md` and `PROJECT_*.md` files outlining the system's architecture, threat models, and gap analysis.

## Summary
Overall, the repository demonstrates a highly modular, advanced Agentic OS focused on local capability execution, robust security controls, and multi-interface user interactions.
